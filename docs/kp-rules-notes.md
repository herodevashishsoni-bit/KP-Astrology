# KP rules — working notes (from source texts)

Raw notes collected while reading. Citations: R1..R6 = Readers, M<year> = magazine volume, followed by line number in texts/extracted.

## Chart casting
- Use Krishnamurti ayanamsa only; other ayanamsas "useless if you want to follow K.P." (R1 preface, line ~60).
- Ephemeris: Raphael's Ephemeris with KP ayanamsa, Raphael's Tables of Houses (= Placidus) (R1 l.436-441).
- Sayana cusps from tables of houses, minus ayanamsa = Nirayana cusps (M1968 l.2788-2867).
- Ayanamsa check values from worked examples:
  - 1913: 22°33' (M1968 l.11827)
  - 1939 (20-5-1939): 22°55' (M1968 l.2867)
  - 1963: 23°15' (M1964 l.12232)
  - 1968: 23°19' (M1968 l.7171, 9935)
- Each star (13°20') divided into 9 subs proportional to Vimshottari dasa years, starting from the star lord (R1 l.411-417).
- Only Vimshottari dasa is used for timing (R1 l.409-410).

## Houses (R2 ch.17 "The twelve houses", lines 9765+)
- House measured cusp to cusp (Placidus bhava), 1st = from Asc cusp to 2nd cusp (l.9774).
- 12th from any house = negation/detriment of that house's matters (l.~10770 "twelfth house to any bhava is negation").
- Derived houses: relatives are read by counting from their house (e.g. 4 = mother, 9 = father, 3 = 1st younger sibling, 11 = elder sibling, 5 = 1st child, 7 = partner). Same house can be good for native and bad for a relative.
- Sibling/child order: 3 = 1st younger sibling, 5 (3rd from 3) = 2nd younger, 7 (3rd from 5) = 3rd younger. 5 = 1st child/conception, 7 = 2nd child, 9 = 3rd child.
- Longevity houses: 1, 3, 8. Marakas = 12th to them: 12, 2, 7. Time of death judged from 2 and 7 "after judging the bhadhaka-sthanas" (l.~10790, 10830).
- Death: if long life promised, evil period in youth gives ill-health, not death (l.10840s).
- Self-caused death: lagna lord in 8, or planet in 8 in star of lagna lord, or lord 8 conjoined lagna lord.
- Place of death by house connected to 8th: 3 = travel nearby; 12 = nursing home/asylum/unknown place; 11 = friend's/elder brother's house; 9 = far-off place/long journey.
- Mode of death by planet: Uranus sudden/explosion/electric/misdiagnosis; Neptune mysterious/coma/drugs/gas/poison/drowning; Saturn chronic/cold/bones, slow; Jupiter peaceful (afflicted: blood poisoning, cancer, liver, lungs); Mars accident/fire/shock/inflammation (fiery sign fire/violence/machines, watery drowning, airy haemorrhage); 8th in watery sign -> drowning.
- 3rd house is maraka to father (Rahu in 3 often causes father's death in its period) (l.~10260).
- Disease: 6 = disease, 12 = hospitalisation, 11 = discharge/recovery. Health breaks only in periods of planets in 6, in stars of occupants of 6, or conjoined lord 6.
- Marriage: 7 principal, plus 2 and 11 (l.~10660). 2nd house also governs second marriage (8th from 7).
- Change of residence: 3 and 7 (also 3 = 12th to 4).
- Children: 5 progeny; fruitful signs Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces; barren signs Aries, Leo, Virgo (on 5th/11th cusps).
- Lands: 4 + Mars favourable -> buy; unfavourable -> sell/loss. Vehicle: 4 + Venus.
- Loans: 6 = borrowing; success in raising loans if benefics in 6.
- Father = 9th always (not 4th/10th) (R2 l.~10930).
- 10th = maraka to father (2nd from 9) and mother (7th from 4); advancement and parent's death can coincide (R2 l.~11260).
- Profession: houses 2, 6, 10 together ("material trinity") (R2 l.~11120).
- 11th: fulfilment of desires, gain, success in all undertakings; lover more from 11 than 7.
- Debts: borrowed money repaid = 5th (11th from 7th); money lent returned = 11th (R2 l.~11450).
- Income sources by house: 1 own exertion, 2 self-acquisition, 3 writing/agency/younger sibling, 4 mother/property/vehicles, 5 speculation/children, 6 service/borrowing, 7 partner/litigation, 8 insurance/legacy/PF, 9 father/foreign/research, 10 profession/inheritance; 12 expenses.
- BADHAKA (R2 l.~11590): movable-sign lagna -> 11th is badhakasthana; fixed -> 9th; common -> 7th. Applies only to longevity/health. During dasa/bhukti of planet owning badhaka (or connected to it, whichever stronger) -> ill-health/anxiety; if long life promised, survives; if allotted span over, badhaka lord/planets in badhaka end life.
- Marakas for ALL natives: 2 and 7.
- 11th = 8th from 4 -> danger/death of mother; 6th from 6 -> recovery; 12th from 12 -> discharge from hospital.
- Saturn beneficial in 11: gives result in its own sub-period and blocks preceding subs (R2 l.~11690).
- Results (good/bad) of a house come only in dasa/bhukti of the planet connected, and on days/stars/lagnas ruled by it (R2 l.~11830).
- KEY: what matters most is not occupation/lordship but the constellation (star) the planet is in and which houses the star lord owns (R2 l.~11900).
- Loan repayment: 12 (6th from 7) and 8 (R2 l.~11870).
- 12th: loss, expenses, investment, hospital, prison, foreign/far place, secret enemies, moksha.

## Sub division (R3 ch.1, l.~1000-1100)
- Star = 800'. Each planet's sub = dasa years x 6'40" (800/120). Sun 40', Moon 1°06'40", Mars/Ketu 46'40", Rahu 2°00', Jupiter 1°46'40", Saturn 2°06'40", Mercury 1°53'20", Venus 2°13'20".
- First sub of a star = star lord, then Vimshottari order (Ke Ve Su Mo Ma Ra Ju Sa Me). 249 sign/star/sub divisions (subs split by sign boundaries).
- Nature of result: star lord indicates the matter; transiting/natal planet is the source; SUB LORD decides favourable or unfavourable (R3 l.~950).
- Star lord ~ dasa lord, sub ~ bhukti lord analogy.

## Transit pinpointing (R3 ch.2, l.~1200-1400)
- During Dasa A / Bhukti B (or B/A), the matters signified materialise when A, B, or Sun/Moon transit a zone that is: sign of A + star of B, or sign of B + star of A, or star of A + sub of B, or star of B + sub of A. "Important events indicated by these two planets cannot happen on other dates." (R3 l.~1330)
- R3 ch.2 also lists per-star body parts, diseases, mental qualities, professions (l.~1100-3220) — usable as descriptive data for "details".

## Role of sub (R3 l.3222+)
- Sign = strength/source quality; star lord = NATURE of result (houses it occupies + owns); sub lord = whether favourable or not (success/failure). Event happens in conjoined period of planet, star lord, sub lord (R3 l.~3420).
- Planet as significator of house N gives result through house-N matters (1 own effort, 3 brothers/publication, 4 mother, 5 speculation/children...).
- Planet in sub of evil planet -> loss/failure; same star in benefic sub -> success.
- Rahu as sub: judge which planet Rahu represents and take that (R3 l.~3640).
- R3 l.~3460-5000: table of all 249 subs (sign-star-sub) with body parts, diseases, character, professions. Can be encoded as descriptive data.

## Sub for detailed reading (R3 l.~5180-5340)
- A planet gives results of its STAR LORD's houses more predominantly than its own occupation/ownership. "Occupants or owners do not offer the results of the houses but the results of the lord of the constellation in which they are deposited."
- Star lord = matter; sub lord = success/failure, delay/denial, union/separation, acquire/dispose. Lordship of the planet itself is not decisive (example: Moon in Venus(2) star but Saturn(6) sub cannot give marriage; Mars lord 8 in Venus star Jupiter sub gives marriage).
- Two planets in same star: one in benefic sub gives the event, other in malefic sub harms it.
- Transit stages: as a significator transits through the subs of another significator's star, the event unfolds stage by stage.

## Behaviour of planets (R3 l.5340+)
- Planet in star of X gives results of: (a) house OCCUPIED by X (predominant), and (b) houses OWNED by X. Occupation stronger than ownership.
- Rahu/Ketu stars: results by house occupied by node, plus its star lord and sign lord.
- Chapter gives per-planet delineations "X as star lord in/of house N" (1..12) — descriptive text data.

## Benefic/malefic in KP (R3 l.6025-6230)
- No planet wholly benefic/malefic; a planet good for the native can be evil for a relative (houses counted from relative's house).
- Benefic for a house = significator not in star AND sub of significators of 6, 8, 12 counted FROM THAT HOUSE (R3 l.~6180). e.g. for 2nd: avoid 6/8/12 from lagna... rule stated: "for each house note whether the significator is posited in the constellation and sub of the significator of houses 6, 8, 12 counted from that particular sign".
- Health: significator of lagna should not signify 6/8/12.
- Example: Jupiter lord 2,5 (Scorpio lagna); planet in Jupiter star with sub = lord 11 -> child; sub = lord 12 -> no child in that period.

## Cusps and subs (R3 l.6235-6380)
- Asc sub lord signifying 1+N gives the matters of N: 1+2 money/service; 1+3 brothers, short travel; 1+4 education, property; 1+5 sport/speculation/children; 1+6 sickness, wins litigation; 1+7 union/marriage; 1+8 danger/accident, legacy; 1+9 long journey, religion, research; 1+10 honour; 1+11 profits, recovery, fulfilment; 1+12 troubles, hospital, imprisonment, isolation.
- 2nd cusp sub lord: sig 1,2,6 or 10 (not 7) -> service; sig 1,7,10 -> business; sig 1,2,12 + Sun/Moon/Venus -> loss of vision; + Saturn -> toothache; 2+3 gain via writing/agency; 2+4 inheritance/property; 2+5 entertainment; 2+6 profit/overdraft; 2+7 partner/marriage gains; 2+8 legacy/insurance (with 9 bank failure loss; with 11 sure gain); 2+9 export/foreign; 2+10 steady progress, govt service; 2+11 gains without effort, lottery; 2+12 earning in hospital/jail etc.

## SIGNIFICATOR DEFINITION (R3 l.6440-6560) — core
- Planets in the star of a planet signify the bhava occupied by that star lord ("Significators of the bhava occupied by the lord of the constellation"). Also owned.
- The deciding factor good/bad is the SUB occupied by the significator.
- Lagna significator in sub of significator of 6 -> illness; of 8 -> danger; of 12 -> runs away/imprisoned/hospitalised.
- Significator of 2: in sub of 6 -> borrows; sub of 8 or 12 -> lends or repays; sub of 11 -> gain (money/child/in-law); sub of 3 -> via brother; sub of 4 -> car/house; sub-lord sig 4+11 -> purchase; sub-lord sig 12 (not 4) -> sells property; sub-lord sig 10+12 -> lets property on rent.
- Significator of 7: in sub of 2 or 11 -> gain/reunion/marriage; sub of 12 -> separation or wife's ill-health; sub of 6 -> wife goes away (separation); sub sig 1+2 -> danger to partner's life; sub of 4 -> partner gains; sub of 5 -> partner gains/pleasure; sub sig 8 -> income or danger to life; if that 8 significator also sig 1 and 7 -> death of partner (8 and 1 are maraka (2nd/7th) to the 7th).
- Judge each relative by its house: mother 4, father 9, child 5.
- If no planet occupies a planet's stars, that planet itself gives the house results strongly (R3 l.~6700, 6770).
- Nodes give results of planets conjoined with them / sign lord they occupy; node can be "stronger than" vacant house's lord (R3 l.~6790).
- Worked natal example (b.28-1-1900? chart l.~6600): father's death = periods of planets signifying 9 whose sub lords are badhaka/maraka TO 9 (2nd & 7th from 9 = 10 & 3; badhaka for 9th sign); mother's death = planets signifying 4 in subs of maraka to 4 (2nd & 7th from 4 = 5 & 10) — died Venus D / Mercury B / Saturn A. Child's death = planets in star of node representing 2nd to 5th, sub of lord 7 to 5 (i.e. 6 & 11 from lagna = marakas of 5th).
- Day-level confirmation in example: death on day of Moon in star where dasa lord was, lagna in relevant star.
- Marriage worked example: significators of 2, 7, 11; married in Venus D (Venus in Ketu star, Ketu stronger than vacant 7th's lord) / Ketu B / Venus A / Moon sookshma.
- Education: dasa lord connected with 4 (education) and 9 (higher education) gives studies; star lord also signifying 12 ends studies.
- Service: houses 2, 6, 10 (first appointment Sun D (in 6) / Saturn B (lord 6)).

## Physical features (R3 l.~6900)
- Height/build: sign occupied by the STAR LORD of the Asc SUB LORD (sign gives general, star modifies, sub decides). Gemini/Leo/Sag/Aqu tall; Ari/Vir/Lib/Sco average; Tau/Can/Cap/Pis short. Lagna lord early in sign tall -> end of sign short.
- Aspects to Asc cusp: Jupiter overweight, Saturn emaciation, Rahu tall, Ketu short, Uranus long limbs.
- Moles: by sign of lagna, lagna lord, Moon, malefics, 6th cusp; side by masculine/feminine sign rules (male: lagna & lord masculine -> right). Degree 0-10 upper third, 10-20 middle, 20-30 lower part.

## LONGEVITY (R3 l.~7080-7300+)
- KP method: Asc nirayana -> sign lord, star lord, sub lord. If Asc SUB LORD is benefic -> long life; malefic -> short. Sub lord decides (twins).
- Benefic lords: 1,5,9,10; malefic lords: 6,8,12 (if lord of 1/5/9/10 also owns 6/8/12 consider mostly benefic).
- Disease: significators of 6 (6th cusp, occupants of 6, planets in star of occupants, lord of 6, planets in star of lord 6) in their periods; significators of 11 give cure.
- Danger to life/cause/place of death: planets in star of evil planets in 8 (except Saturn), occupants of 8, planets in star or sub of lord 8, lord of 8th cusp.
- Judge 1, 8, 3 (longevity) then 2, 7, 12 (maraka/moksha).
- Steps: classify span (short 0-33, middle 33-66, long 66-100); then find dasa running in that age band covering maraka planets; confirm by transit of significators over sensitive points; confirm with ruling planets.
- Significator strength order (stated for badhaka/maraka, R3 l.~7270): (1) planets in star of OCCUPANTS, (2) occupants, (3) planets in star of LORD, (4) lord; plus planets conjoined/aspected by them.
- Kendradhipatya not to be used for longevity decision.
- Maraka analysis same order: planets in star of maraka occupants, occupants, planets in star of maraka lords, lords, conjoined/aspected.
- Longevity Ex.1 (R3 l.~7300): Scorpio lagna, purna ayush promised; nodes represent sign lords (Rahu in Venus sign = Venus); "tenant stronger than owner". Death predicted Mercury D / Rahu B / Ketu A (2-1-70 to 25-2-70); exact day by transit: Saturn in Ketu star, Saturn sub, conjoining Ketu (antara lord), opposing Rahu (bhukti lord). Died 18-2-70.
- Longevity Ex.2 (R3 l.~7340): Cancer lagna; badhaka 11 (Taurus); Neptune not allotted stars (ignore for star-lordship). Planet with no planets in its stars is itself a significator. When many planets in one star, the sub decides which are evil. Choose dasa (running) + bhukti of strongest malefic + sub-sub of other significators; transit of Sun over star/sub of significators gives week.
- "Udu Dasa" = Vimshottari (star-based) dasa.

## Mode / place of death (R3 l.~7480-7560)
- Traditional yogas listed but "take as general principle only".
- KP: danger during conjoined periods of (a) sign lord, star lord, sub lord of 8th CUSP and star lord of that sub lord; or (b) sign lord, star lord, sub lord and star lord of the position of LORD OF 8.
- If sub (of those) is a benefic promising long life/cure -> escape/survive; if sub lord is malefic and significator of death -> succumbs.
- Nature of end by sub lord: Jupiter pleasant; Mars sudden; Saturn chronic/lingering.
- Place: 8th cusp sub lord signifies 1, 4, 10 -> own place; 3 -> during short journey (mostly return); 6, 8 or 12 -> jail/hospital/unknown place; 9 -> long journey/far-off place.
- Benefics in 8 / 8th cusp well aspected -> peaceful end; Saturn in 8 prolonged ailment/fall; Uranus sudden (accident, electrocution, air crash); Neptune poison/drowning.
- Air-crash example: 9th significators (long journey) + 8th cusp sub lord connected to 3/9 -> death far away. Spouse's death read from wife's chart with 7th cusp as husband's lagna (derived houses).

## Accidents (R3 l.~7570)
- Gemini/Virgo lagnas prone (Mars/Saturn own 6/8); Virgo worst (Mars lord 8). Accident in D/B/A of lords of 12 (hospital), 8 (danger), 6. Jupiter aspecting lagna/dasa lord protects.

## HEALTH / DISEASE (R3 l.~7620-7720)
- Asc cusp in star of lord 6 or 8 -> low immunity. Asc SUB LORD in star of significator of 6 -> sickly.
- Asc, lagna lord, and sub in stars of significators of houses other than 6/8/12 -> long life, good health. Cusp more vital than lord.
- Planets in star or sub of lord 6 cause disease. Disease occurs in period of planet connected with 6; cure in sub-period of planet connected with Asc or 11.
- No cure from chronic disease if: no planet in 11, no planet in star of owner/occupant of 11, and 11th cusp and lord 11 in sub of evil planets.
- Nature of disease: sign (body part, by house from lagna too), star lord (nature e.g. Saturn chronic), sub lord (Venus fibroid tumour, Jupiter cancer, Mars surgery, Moon pustulation). Movable signs short duration; common = relapse; fixed = chronic.

## FINANCE / FORTUNE (R3 l.~7720-8500)
- Rajayogas/traditional yogas rejected as unreliable (R3 l.~7960).
- KP wealth significators, strongest first: (a) planets in star of OCCUPANTS of 2, 6, 10, 11 — improve status in their periods if in SUB of significators of 2, 6 or 11; (b) occupants of 2, 6, 10, 11 in sub of significators of 2/6/11; (c) planets in star of LORDS of 2, 6, 10, 11 and in sub of sigs of 2/6/11; (d) the lords themselves in sub of sigs of 2/6/11; (e) planets conjoined/aspected by them.
- Condition: sub governed by significators of 2/6/11 and not adversely aspected. Evil sub lord -> loss in the conjoined period of planet and sub lord.
- Majority of planets in star or sub of lord 8 or 12 -> loss/poverty, cheques to repay loans.
- Planet owning both good (11) and evil (8/12) houses: in its dasa, gains in bhuktis of benefics (sigs of 2,6,11), loss in others — both happen.
- Movable signs/angles/harmonious aspects = fortunate.
- Recovery of lost wealth: lord 7 in sub of lord 2 (KP).
- Fortune (service) example R3 l.~8400: houses 2 (bank/self-acquisition), 6 (exertion/loan), 10 (profession/inheritance). Sun = karaka profession, Jupiter = bank position. Fortuna gives results of its star lord in the conjoined period of its star lord and sub lord ("Always take the Bhava and not the signs").
- Movable sign significator -> changes/transfers.
- Nodes: if Rahu/Ketu occupies a sign owned by a significator, the node is STRONGER than that planet (R3 l.~8590, 8680 "nodes are stronger than planets"; "Rahu or Kethu will ever be stronger than the lord of the house in which they are posited and also the planets with which they are conjoined" l.~8890). Node gives results of sign lord + conjoined planets.
- TRANSIT RULE (editor's ruling, R3 l.~8560): in Rahu dasa / Saturn bhukti, results come when significators transit Rahu's star in Saturn's sign (Satabhisha); i.e. star of dasa lord in sign of bhukti lord.
- Father's death example: Saturn bhukti (Saturn in star of lord 10 = maraka to 9; Saturn = badhaka lord for 9th sign) — father died; day: Moon transiting Saturn's sign in star of Sun (karaka for father).

## PROFESSION / PROMOTION timing examples (R3 l.~8590-8680) — significators of 10 (planets in star of lord 10 when 10 vacant), node in sign of lord 10 strongest.

## RETIREMENT (R3 l.~8680)
- Service = 2, 6, 10; 12th from these = 1, 5, 9 = end of service / retirement.
- Take significators of 1, 5, 9; at retirement age choose the running dasa/bhukti and the strongest significator as antara; date by Sun transiting dasa lord's sign, bhukti lord's star, antara lord's sub.

## LOTTERY / SPECULATION / RACES (R3 l.~8700-8960)
- Houses 1, 2, 3, 6, 10, 11 favourable -> victory, litigation success, competition win, lottery gain. 5 = mind/opportunity to speculate. Planets in 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 12 adverse (loss).
- 6th = 12th to 7th (competitors) -> loss to opponent = gain to native.
- Sources of income indicated by the SUB LORD of the planet.
- Gain by speculation requires dasa lord connected with 5 and 2 (and 6/11).
- Day: during dasa of X, Jupiter transiting the sign of the dasa lord (in sub of dasa lord) gives favourable result. Transit of dasa/bhukti lords considered first.
- Sade-sati rejected as meaningless (R3 l.~8740).

## DEBT / LOANS (R3 l.~8960-9100+)
- 6 = raising loan; 12 = repayment/expenses; 8 = 2nd from 7 (lender's finance): sub lord of 8th cusp sig 5, 6, 8, 12 -> heavy debts; sig 2, 10 or 11 -> repays with pleasure.
- Receipt of money: 2, 6, 10, 11. Discharge of debts: 4, 5, 8, 12.
- Planets in 2, 10, 11 in sub of sigs of 4/5/8/12 reduce income; planets in 4,5,8,12 in such subs increase expenses, block repayment.
- Cause of debt: sub lord of 6 also sig 12 & associated with 6/8/12 -> ill-health/partner expenses/loss; lord 2 sig 6/8/12 -> family expenses, eye/throat treatment...
- Reason for debt by house of significator associated with 6/8/12 (R3 l.~9100-9200): 2 family/eye-throat treatment/partner danger; 3 brother (male sign)/sister (female sign)/surety/journeys/press; 4+Moon mother/cattle, 4+Mars land/buildings, lord 4 with 3/8/12 = disposal of property; 5+Jupiter children/speculation/love; 6+Saturn enemies/theft/disease, +Jupiter litigation, +Ju/Ma/Su election; 7+lords 2/10 business partner, 7+Venus wife, 7+Jupiter litigation; 8+malefic enemies/disease; 9 father/long journey/higher studies; 10+Sun father's debts/business; 11 surety to friend, 11+Mars elder brother, 11+Sun paternal uncle, 11+Ju+Ve son-in-law; 12+Saturn theft/fraud/secret enemies.
- Debts cleared in periods of significators of 4, 5, 8, 12 (2,6,10,11 from 7th) and occupants/owners of 2,6,10,11 (beneficial).
- Imprisonment/loss of reputation over debts: malefics in 2, 5, 9, 12.
- Free-from-debts example (R3 l.~9500): planet in star of lord 6 and sub of lord 12 -> borrow-repay cycle; planet in 11 in star of lord 11 and benefic sub -> no debts. "'A' dasa, 'B' bhukti, 'A' antara will offer the result to the full extent" (R3 l.~9560) -> use for scoring.

## SIBLINGS (R3 l.9909-10117)
- KP: sub lord of the 3rd cusp decides positive/negative for 3rd house matters. Plurality of brothers/sisters only if 3rd cusp sub lord is in star of a planet in a DUAL sign, or itself in a dual sign, or 3rd cusp aspected by planet in dual sign. No single rule found yet for number/sex (KP research ongoing).
- Longevity of next younger sibling: take 3rd CUSP as ascendant of the younger and recompute other cusps for that latitude (i.e. Placidus houses with Asc = 3rd cusp), then judge per KP longevity rules.
- Co-borns promised if significator of 3 is connected with 2 and 11.
- Danger to brother: sub lord of 3 is significator of 8 or 10 (8th from 3 = 10).
- Brother lucky: significator of 4 also sig of 12 and 1 (i.e. 2nd, 10th, 11th from 3).
- Enmity with brothers: significator of 1 also sig of 3, 6, 8. Harmony: significator of 3 also sig of 11 and 1.
- Which brother dies: when many siblings, sibling whose ruling planets (at their birth) match the evil dasa/bhukti lords passes away in that period.
- Partition of property: conjoined periods of significators of 3, 9, 12 and 4 or 10. Property difficulty in periods of lords of 2, 4, 6, 8, 12.
- Acquires brother's property: lord 3 strong in star of significator of 2. Expenses via younger siblings: 3rd connected with lord 12.

## MOTHER (R3 l.10117-10230)
- 4th house ALONE for mother (not 10th). Judge with 4th as mother's lagna; houses counted from 4th (5, 1, 2 from lagna = her 2, 10, 11 = possessions/income; 9th = her 6th = disease/debts). Same dasa periods apply.
- Badhaka, kendradhipatya, marakas counted FROM the 4th indicate evil planets for her longevity.
- KP: if 4th cusp and Moon are in star of significators of 9th, 11th or 3rd (6th, 8th, 12th from 4) and sub belongs to badhaka lord or maraka lord of 4th -> mother unhealthy, won't live long after birth. Death of mother: take 4th cusp as her lagna.
- Day birth: Sun = father, Venus = mother; night birth: Saturn = father, Moon = mother (karakas).

## HOUSE / PROPERTY / VEHICLE / EDUCATION (R3 l.10232+)
- 4th + Mars = buildings/land (Mars & Moon = land); 4th + Venus = vehicle; 4th + Moon = mother; 4th + Mercury = education.
- "Connected with Mars" = in Mars's sign, Mars's star, or Mars's sub.
- House significators order: (1) planets occupying 4th in Mars star/sub, (2) planets in star of such occupants, (3) lord of 4 in star/sub of Mars, (4) planets in star of such lords, (5) Mars favourably placed to 4.
- Mode: 4th + lord 10 good aspect -> inheritance; 4th + lords 1 & 12 -> buys with own funds; 4th + lords 1, 6, 12 -> borrows to buy (loan/co-op); lord 1 sig 2 & 6 not connected with Mars -> mortgage; lord 4 + lord 8 connected (lord 1 not) -> SELLS property; lord 4 + lord 12 + Sun -> loss by fire/auction.
- Evil planet in 4th -> loss of a house already owned, in its periods.
- Do NOT split a dasa into first-half/second-half; planet owning good and bad houses gives good results in bhuktis of benefic subs and bad in evil subs.
- Construction material/rooms/tenants by planet (descriptive).
- PURCHASE OF HOUSE (R3 l.~10650): houses 4 (possession), 11 (acquisition/gain), 12 (payment). Taking possession also needs 6 or 9 (12th from 7 seller; 12th from 10 = seller's 4th). Significators: planets in star of occupants, occupants, then owners/aspecting. Example: purchase in Venus D / Sun B / Jupiter A; day confirmed via ruling planets of query moment & transit of Sun/Venus over Moon's query degree.
- ACQUISITION of house: 2, 4, 11. DISPOSAL (sale): 3, 5, 10 (12th from 4, 6, 11) (R3 l.~10720).
- **IMPORTANT REVISION (R3 l.~10725): "In the research after 1967 it was found that we have to take always the Lagna alone. Never judge whether Moon sign or Lagna is stronger" — twin births differ only by cusps. Use Lagna/cusps only.**
- Strength reminders: node conjoined with a planet is stronger than it; node in sign of a planet represents it and is stronger; node aspected by planet gives that planet's results.
- Mars in star of lord 11 -> no desperate sale (sale at satisfactory terms).
- "Guru balam" (Jupiter transit strength) not used.

## VEHICLE (R3 l.~10900-11100)
- 4th + Venus (karaka) primary; "Vahana yoga" judged from houses 4, 9, 10, 11 + beneficial Venus, Moon or Jupiter.

### Nodes — the KP view (R2 p.452–466). KEY
- Nodes give results in this order: (a) planets conjoined with them; (b) planets aspecting them; (c) the lord of the sign they occupy. They also act as agents of their sign lord, star lord and sub lord, and give the combined result of the three (R2 p.452, 462).
- A node is stronger than the planet it is conjoined with; it gives that planet's results more than the planet itself does (R2 p.461).
- A node in a sign gives the results of both signs owned by that sign lord (R2 p.462–463).
- A node is a benefic if it is conjoined with or aspected by lords of favourable houses, or if it is in the star or sub of such a lord (R2 p.460).
- A node in the star of lords of 6, 8 or 12 gives undesirable results, whatever house it occupies (R2 p.460).
- DEATH: a node causes death in its own period or sub period if it is in the star of the planet occupying the badhakasthana, or in the star of the badhaka lord when no planet occupies it; also if it is in the sub of the badhaka lord, or in the star of the lords of 2 and 7, or conjoined with or aspected by them. This holds "irrespective of the house it occupies" (R2 p.461, Libra-lagna example).
- PROFESSION: a node conjoined with or aspected by the lord of 2, 6 or 10 → service begins in the node's sub periods, if its star lord is also a significator (R2 p.462).
- MARRIAGE: the lord of 2/7/11, or a planet in 2/7/11, aspecting a node → marriage in the node's sub period, if its star lord is a significator (R2 p.462).
- CHILDREN: a node in 2, 5 or 11 with good aspects → children in its periods (R2 p.462).
- Example: Rahu 26°36′ Taurus (Venus sign, Mars star, Jupiter sub) → manager in an automobile industry; Rahu dasa, Jupiter bhukti, Venus antara, Mars sookshma (R2 p.463). This shows the dasa, bhukti, antara and sookshma lords all being the node's sign, star and sub lords.

- Uttara Kalamrita rules on nodes in 5/9 with maraka lords are quoted (traditional) (R2 p.452–454). KP supersedes them with the star-lord rules above. Node ownership and exaltation are contradictory across sages, so KP says ignore them (R2 p.455).

- Aspects to a bhava: use the cusp only, not the whole bhava (R2 p.471).

## Reader 5 (Transit / Gocharapala Nirnayam)
- **Scope:** PDF pages 37–186 duplicate R3 pp.15–164, as the book's own note says. Verified by matching text at pages 37, 60, 100, 140, 184, 185 and 186. I read pages 1–36 and 187–365 in full.
- **Traditional gochara (from the Moon sign: per-planet good houses, vedha, Saturn/Jupiter death rules, chandrashtama, sade-sati, ashtama sani, Guru-bala):** reported and **rejected** (R5 p.36, 246–264, 327–331). The death-transit rules "answer any year" → useless.
- **Lagna is primary, not the Moon or the Sun** (R5 intro, p.172). Fortuna = Asc + Moon − Sun. KSK says to consider it in the intro, but it is not used in any KP method later. Treat it as **optional/descriptive only**.
- **Western transit rules** (aspects, orbs ±8°, progressions): reported; "not included in my system" (R5 p.25). Excluded.
- **Core KP transit rule (R5 p.156–161, 171–187, 265):**
  - A planet transiting any of the 3 stars of planet X gives the matters X signifies (by occupation and ownership).
  - The **source/route** comes from the transiting planet's own signification.
  - **Success or failure** comes from the **sub lord** of the transited point.
  - Each sub marks a stage of the matter. Example sequence for a marriage: advert → girl ill → boys come → jewels → negotiation → fixation → purchases → **wedding in the sub of a significator** → separation.
- **Dasa–transit agreement (R5 p.161–163, 187):** for A dasa B bhukti C antara, the sensitive points are every sign/star/sub permutation of A, B and C (e.g. A sign–B star–C sub, B sign–A star–C sub, C sign–A star–B sub …). Also A star–B sub, and B star–A sub.

  The event comes when one of these points is transited by:
  - the dasa, bhukti or antara lords
  - the Sun or the Moon
  - the **Ascendant at the native's current place of residence** (not the birthplace unless they still live there)
- **Nodes on the day (R5 p.163):** a node gives results through, in order: the planet conjoined with it, the lord of its natal star, its sign lord. The weekday follows the same order. A Rahu period event may fall in Rahu-kalam (Tamil Nadu). Rahu-kalam is minor; flag it.
- **Dasa-level transit of the Sun (R5 p.190):** before subs were used, KSK timed A dasa B bhukti to the fortnight when the Sun transits A's sign with B's star. This was superseded by star = dasa and sub = bhukti; each period then has 3 zodiac points 120° apart.
- **Retrograde transits (R5 p.287, 322):**
  - retrograde in an evil sub → malefic results aggravated
  - retrograde in a benefic sub → gains, but smaller
  - **stationary = severest**
